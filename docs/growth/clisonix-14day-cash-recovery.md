# Clisonix 14-Day Cash Recovery Plan (Founder Solo)

## Goal
Generate first paid cash flow in 14 days without investors.

## North-Star Target
- Day 14 target: **1-2 paying clients**
- Minimum cash-in target: **€500-€2,000**
- Priority: speed of cash, not perfect product

## Offer Stack (Simple, Fast, Sellable)
Choose 1 primary offer and 1 backup offer.

### Offer A (Primary): AI Automation Setup Sprint
- Delivery: 48-72h
- Price: €300 starter / €700 standard
- Includes:
  - intake call (30 min)
  - 1 workflow automation (lead capture, follow-up, reporting)
  - handover video + SOP

### Offer B (Backup): Tracking + Funnel Fix
- Delivery: 24-48h
- Price: €200 starter / €500 standard
- Includes:
  - UTM + simple conversion tracking setup
  - landing CTA cleanup
  - weekly dashboard template

## Non-Negotiable Rules
- No free full project work
- No custom proposal before discovery call
- 50% upfront for projects under €1,000
- If no reply in 48h -> follow-up + move on

---

## Daily Activity Targets (Days 1-14)
- 20 outbound messages/day
- 5 follow-ups/day
- 2 discovery calls/day target
- 1 proposal/day target
- 1 close every 5-7 proposals

## Daily KPI Tracker
- Sent
- Replies
- Calls booked
- Calls completed
- Proposals sent
- Deals won
- Cash collected

---

## 14-Day Execution

### Day 1
- Finalize one-page offer (A + B)
- Prepare payment link/invoice template
- Make a simple portfolio proof sheet (3 examples, even internal)

### Day 2
- Build prospect list (100 leads total)
  - local businesses
  - B2B startups
  - founders with weak funnel/automation

### Day 3
- Send first 20 targeted messages
- Run first 5 follow-ups from warm contacts

### Day 4
- Send second 20 messages
- Book minimum 2 calls

### Day 5
- Execute calls, propose same day
- Push for 50% upfront

### Day 6
- Delivery sprint for first client (if closed)
- If no close: increase to 30 messages/day

### Day 7
- Weekly review:
  - Which niche replies most?
  - Which message converts best?
  - Remove low-response segment

### Days 8-10
- Double down on best niche + best message
- Keep 20-30 outbound/day
- Focus on closing, not broad brainstorming

### Days 11-12
- Ask delivered clients for testimonial + referral
- Offer small referral bonus or add-on

### Days 13-14
- Close pending leads
- Collect final payments
- Build 30-day continuation pipeline

---

## Emergency Pivot (If no client by Day 7)
- Narrow niche to one vertical only
- Reduce starter package price by 20-30% for first 3 clients
- Offer "48h delivery" as urgency advantage
- Increase outbound to 40/day for 3 days

## Cash Protection
- Keep monthly tools near zero unless required
- Do not start ad spend until first organic/project revenue lands
- Reinvest max 20-30% of first month revenue into growth

## Weekly Reflection Prompt
- What generated replies?
- What got ignored?
- What closed?
- What can be removed immediately?
